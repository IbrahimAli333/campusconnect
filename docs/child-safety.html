Unibridge Child Safety Standards

Unibridge is an academic and professional networking app intended for university communities. It is for adults only: everyone must confirm they are 18 or older when they create an account, and it is not directed at children. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are deleted. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. See also our Terms of Service.

Our standards

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors is strictly prohibited on Unibridge.
  • Grooming, predatory behavior, and any attempt to contact or solicit minors through the platform is prohibited.
  • Accounts that violate these standards are removed, and where required we report violations to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the applicable local authority.

In-app reporting and moderation

  • Every profile and every post in Unibridge can be reported directly in the app ("Report" on the detail panel). Reports go to our moderation team for review.
  • Users can block other users at any time; blocking hides profiles, posts, and messages in both directions and stops further contact.
  • Reported content is reviewed by a human moderator. Accounts involved in child safety violations are deactivated as soon as the violation is confirmed, which signs them out and hides their profile, posts, and messages from everyone.

Compliance with child safety laws

Unibridge complies with applicable child safety laws in the regions where the app is available, including obligations to remove and report CSAM.
