Delete Your Unibridge Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Unibridge account and the data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Unibridge app and log in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Unibridge account". Requests are processed within 7 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your account record, profile (including skills and portfolio entries), opportunity posts, applications, saved opportunities, connections and connection requests, messages you sent, content reports you filed, blocks, and push notification registrations.

What may be kept

No personal data is retained after account deletion. Standard server logs (which may include IP addresses but no account content) are kept for a short operational period and then discarded.
