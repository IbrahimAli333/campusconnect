Delete your Unibridge account

Android 1.0.0 — Azerbaijan release

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in and open Me.
  2. Find Delete Account and select Delete account.
  3. Enter your account password when prompted.
  4. Select Permanently delete to confirm.

If that flow is unavailable for your account, request deletion by email instead.

Request deletion by email

Email ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com with the subject “Unibridge account deletion”. Include the email address used for your Unibridge account and state that you want the account deleted. You do not need to reinstall the app. Do not send your password, sign-in codes or identity documents.

We handle account-deletion requests within seven days. We must confirm account ownership before irreversible deletion and may contact the email address already recorded for the account to confirm your request. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us; do not send someone else's credentials or documents.

What account deletion removes

The account and its related network profile, skills, resume entries, owned opportunities and related applications, connections, sent and received messages, saved items, reports, blocks and backend push-token records are removed from the active application database. The operation also deletes announcements authored by the account and legacy teaching-material listings attributed to its teacher profile. Shared courses and other users' unrelated content remain.

What is handled separately

Files or pages hosted at external material links, historical content whose author reference was already removed, and support correspondence may need separate handling. Tell us which item your request concerns so we can identify it without removing another person's content. Account deletion does not recall recipient-held copies, such as screenshots or delivered notifications.

Database recovery copies, operational logs and notification-provider identifiers have separate retention processes. Render currently provides a three-day database recovery window and a seven-day Hobby dashboard-log policy. These are not guarantees that every physical copy is destroyed on those dates. Deleting a backend push token does not automatically delete the corresponding provider identifiers.

Notifications already submitted for delivery cannot be recalled by account deletion. Undelivered Android notifications may remain queued with the delivery provider for up to four weeks under the current delivery settings. They can contain a name, an opportunity title or a message preview. Notifications already delivered can remain on the recipient's device until removed there. This delivery window is separate from backend push-token deletion and provider identifier retention.

The seven-day request-handling commitment is separate from these retention processes. We do not promise erasure of every backup, log or provider copy within seven days.
