Delete Your Unibridge Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Unibridge account and the data associated with it. You do not need to accept updated terms to delete your account.

Before you delete: download your data (optional)

In the app, open the Me tab and tap Download my data to save a copy of your account, profile, skills, portfolio, posts, applications, connections, and messages (JSON format).

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Unibridge app and log in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, or, if you signed up with your university Google account, tap Verify with Google and delete.
  5. Tap Permanently delete. You can tap Cancel instead at any point before this step.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app (or prefer not to use it), email ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my Unibridge account". Requests are processed within 7 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that email address so nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes from our live database:

  • your account record (email, name, password hash, role) and your Terms/Privacy acceptance record;
  • your profile, including skills and portfolio/resume entries;
  • your opportunity posts, together with the applications and saves other users made on them;
  • your applications and saved opportunities;
  • your connections and connection requests (sent and received);
  • messages you sent and received — they disappear for the other person too;
  • content reports you filed and reports filed about you;
  • blocks you made and blocks other users made against you;
  • push notification registrations for all your devices.
