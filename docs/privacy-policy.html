Unibridge ("the app") is an academic and professional networking app for university communities, operated by Ibrahim Ali Khudiyev, an individual ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

Who can use Unibridge

Unibridge is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and you confirm this when you sign up. The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. If you believe a minor is using Unibridge, contact us using the details below.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, full name, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), your campus role (member, student, teacher, or another role granted by an administrator), and whether your account is active.
  • Consent record: which version of the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy you accepted, when you accepted it, and when you confirmed you are 18 or older. We do not ask for or store your date of birth.
  • Google sign-in (where available): if you sign in with your university Google account, we receive your email address, name, and whether Google has verified the email. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Google data.
  • Profile information you choose to add: headline, bio, university, faculty, graduation year, location, profile visibility, skills and skill levels, and portfolio/resume entries (education, work, projects, research, awards, certifications, with optional dates, organisation, description, and link). All of these are optional. If you sign up with a recognised university email domain, your university is filled in automatically; you can change it.
  • Activity you create: opportunity posts, applications (with an optional note), saved opportunities, connection requests (with an optional message), messages to accepted connections (and when they were read), content reports you file, and users you block.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, a device push token, your device platform (iOS or Android), and the app language you chose (Azerbaijani, English, or Russian) so notifications arrive in your language. The app explains notifications and asks before showing your phone's permission dialog. You can turn push notifications off at any time in the Me tab, which removes the token from our servers.
  • Opportunity Assistant (optional, where available): the text you type into the assistant.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: our servers and hosting provider record standard request logs (which include IP addresses and the address of the request). To limit sign-in abuse, the server briefly keeps failed sign-in attempts (IP address and the email address tried) in memory for up to one hour; this is not written to the database. If crash reporting is enabled in your version of the app, error reports and performance samples are sent to Sentry (see below).
  • On your device: the app stores your sign-in session, language choice, push notification preference, and whether you have seen the notification prompt in the device's secure storage (or in the browser's local storage on the web).

The app does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, advertising identifiers, or browsing history, contains no advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service (performance of our contract with you): creating and securing your account, showing your profile to other signed-in users according to your visibility setting, showing and recommending opportunities and people (recommendations are calculated from your skills, role, university, faculty, and portfolio keywords; there is no automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect), delivering applications, connection requests, messages, and notifications, and providing your data export.
  • To keep the community safe (our legitimate interests and legal obligations): reviewing reports, enforcing blocks and our Terms, preventing abuse and fraud, and reporting illegal content where required.
  • To keep the app working (our legitimate interests): diagnosing crashes and errors and measuring performance.
  • To record your consent (legal obligation and legitimate interests): keeping proof of which Terms and Privacy Policy version you accepted.

Who can see your information

Other signed-in Unibridge users can see your name, role, and profile according to your visibility setting: public (all signed-in users), university only (signed-in users with the same university), or private (only you). New profiles start as public; you can change this in the Me tab. When you apply to a post, the poster sees your profile summary, skills, portfolio highlights, and your application note. People you message see your messages. Profiles are not visible to people who are not signed in, and we do not publish them on the open web.

Unibridge administrators can see reports, including the reporter's name, and can deactivate accounts. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep information

  • While your account is active, we keep your account, profile, and activity so the service can work. You can edit or delete individual profile fields, skills, portfolio entries, posts, and applications at any time.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately and permanently delete from our live database: your account, consent record, profile, skills, portfolio entries, posts (and the applications and saves other users made on them), your applications, saved posts, connections and connection requests, messages you sent and received (they disappear for the other person too), reports you filed, reports about you, blocks, and push notification registrations.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and portability: download a copy of your data at any time from the Me tab (Download my data), in JSON format.
  • Correction: edit your profile, skills, and portfolio in the Me tab. To change your name or email, contact us.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the Me tab (Delete Account) or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Visibility: choose public, university-only, or private in the Me tab.
  • Push notifications: turn them off in the Me tab or in your device settings.
  • Objection, restriction, and other rights: depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including to object to processing, to restrict it, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Blocking and reporting

You can report any profile or post from its detail view and block any user. Blocking hides that user's profile, posts, and messages from you and yours from them. Reports are reviewed by the Unibridge team; the reported person is not told who reported them. See the Terms of Service for what happens to content and accounts that break our rules.

Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted Argon2 hashes. Sign-in sessions use short-lived tokens, and repeated failed sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
