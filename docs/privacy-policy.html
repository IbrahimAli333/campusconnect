Unibridge Privacy Policy

Android 1.0.0 — Azerbaijan release

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Unibridge is operated by Ibrahim Ali Khudiyev. For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com. Unibridge is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

What we collect and why

We use your name, email address, account identifier, role and account dates to create and manage your account. Passwords are stored as hashes, and authentication tokens maintain your signed-in session.

If Google sign-in is available in your app and you choose it, we receive a Google identity token and use Google's verification service to check it. We process the email address, email-verification status and name, when provided, to create or access your Unibridge account. The identity token is sent to Google for verification; we do not receive your Google password.

Your profile may include your headline, biography, university, faculty, graduation year, a location you type, skills, resume entries and visibility settings. We use profile roles, education, skills and profile text to recommend people and opportunities. You choose which optional profile details to provide. The profile location field is manually entered.

We process opportunities, applications and their status, saved items, connection requests, messages and read status, reports and blocks to provide these features and address abuse. Our server processes message content; messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

For push notifications, we process device delivery identifiers, including registered push tokens. Notification content can include a person's name, an opportunity title or a message preview. Android settings control notification display. Disabling display does not necessarily remove delivery identifiers already held by providers.

We also process technical information for service operation and security. This includes IP addresses and login email addresses used to limit sign-in attempts, and operational error information. Push-delivery error logs can include a push token. If you email us, we process your address and correspondence to handle your request.

Who can receive your information

Members receive profile and opportunity information according to the app's visibility and access rules. Your account email is included in profile information available to members permitted to view it. New network profiles default to public. Choose your visibility settings carefully and avoid including sensitive information in public fields.

Message and application recipients receive the information you send to them. Administrators receive reports for moderation.

Render hosts our API and database in Oregon, United States. Expo processes push tokens and notification content and forwards Android notifications through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service. Google's delivery infrastructure operates globally; push processing is not limited to Oregon. Our support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Your information can therefore be processed outside Azerbaijan.

Our public privacy and account-deletion pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed into GitHub.

How we protect information

Our production API and notification-delivery connections use HTTPS. Password hashing and authenticated access help protect account operations. These protections do not make public profile information private or prevent recipients from keeping copies of content you send them.

Retention and deletion

Account and associated network information is kept to provide the service until deleted. To delete your account in the app, open Me, find Delete Account, select Delete account and follow the confirmation steps shown. You can also request deletion by emailing ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com with your account email address and the subject “Unibridge account deletion”. You do not need to reinstall the app to make an email request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or identity documents.

We handle account-deletion requests within seven days. We must confirm that the request comes from the account holder before irreversible action. The seven-day commitment concerns handling your request; it is not a guarantee that every backup, log or provider copy is erased within seven days.

The account-deletion operation removes the account and related network profile, skills, resume entries, owned opportunities and related applications, connections, sent and received messages, saves, reports, blocks and backend push-token records from the active application database. The operation also deletes announcements authored by the account and legacy teaching-material listings attributed to its teacher profile. Deleting a listing does not delete a file or page hosted at its external link. Shared courses and other users' unrelated content remain. Historical content whose author reference was removed before this cleanup was introduced may need separate identification; contact us if your request concerns such an item. Recipient-held screenshots are not recalled by account deletion.

Render currently provides a three-day database recovery window and a seven-day Hobby dashboard-log retention period. These describe recovery availability and log policy, rather than a guarantee of destruction of every physical provider copy. Notification content passes through Expo's delivery queues; push tokens and Google's installation identifiers follow separate provider retention processes. Deleting your Unibridge account does not automatically erase these provider identifiers. Support correspondence is separate from the app database; contact us about information contained in that correspondence.

Notifications already submitted for delivery cannot be recalled by account deletion. Undelivered Android notifications may remain queued with the delivery provider for up to four weeks under the current delivery settings. They can contain a name, an opportunity title or a message preview. Notifications already delivered can remain on the recipient's device until removed there. This delivery window is separate from backend push-token deletion and provider identifier retention.

Your choices and requests

You can edit available profile fields, change profile visibility, report or block users and content, and request account deletion. Manage notification display in Android settings. Email ibrahimaliworkacc@gmail.com for questions, corrections or other requests concerning your information. Tell us if your request also concerns historical content whose author reference was already removed, external linked resources or support correspondence so that those items can be identified separately.
